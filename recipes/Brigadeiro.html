Traditional Brazillian Brigadeiro

A brazillian classic treat! Every brazillian birthday party must have brigadeiros. It's even more important than the cake, seriously!

By the way, it's very easy to make!

Fun fact: Created in 1945, this treat was made to raise campaign funds for Brazilian presidential candidate and Air Force Brigadier (Brigadeiro) Eduardo Gomes. Due to post-WWII ingredient shortages, supporters used condensed milk and chocolate. He lost the election, but the "Brigadier's candy" became Brazil's most famous dessert!

Ingredients:

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter (plus a little extra for greasing hands)
  • 3 tablespoons cocoa powder (or sweetened chocolate powder)
  • 1 cup chocolate sprinkles (for coating)

Instructions

  1. Combine ingredients: In a medium saucepan, mix the sweetened condensed milk, butter, and cocoa powder before turning on the heat.
  2. Cook: Place the pan over medium-low heat. Stir continuously with a silicone spatula or wooden spoon, ensuring you scrape the bottom and sides constantly so the bottom doesn't burn.
  3. Find the right consistency (The Pan Test): Cook for about 10 to 15 minutes. The mixture will thicken significantly into a dense fudge. You will know it is ready to roll when you drag your spatula through the middle of the pan and the mixture takes about 3 to 5 seconds to flow back together, clearly revealing the bottom of the pan.
  4. Cool: Pour the hot, thick mixture onto a buttered plate. Let it cool completely to room temperature (you can place it in the fridge for about an half-hour to speed up the process).
  5. Roll: Lightly grease your hands with a little butter. Scoop small amounts of the cooled fudge (about the size of a ping-pong ball or 1 tablespoon) and roll them between your palms into smooth balls.
  6. Coat and serve: Toss each ball into a bowl of chocolate sprinkles until fully coated. For the classic presentation, place each Brigadeiro into a mini paper cupcake liner.
