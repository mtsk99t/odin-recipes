Carrot Cake Recipe

Here's the carrot cake recipe. It's the Brazilian version, which my mom has made for a long time. It's fluffy and intensely sweet!

It's easy to make, and the ingredients are affordable!

You will need:

For the cake

  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 3 medium carrots, grated
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk

For the creamy chocolate glaze

  • 1 can(14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can(7.6 oz) table cream OR ¾ cup heavy whipping cream
  • 3 tablespoons chocolate milk powder or cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Instructions

For the cake

  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat your oven to 350°F (180°C) and grease a cake pan with butter and a little flour.
  2. Create the emulsion (The secret for a fluffy cake!): In a blender, add the eggs and the oil. Blend on medium-high speed for 1 to 2 minutes until the mixture becomes pale, slightly frothy, and creamy (similar to a mayonnaise base). This process traps air and ensures a super light and fluffy cake.
  3. Blend the carrots: Gradually add the grated (or chopped) carrots and milk to the blender and blend until the mixture is completely smooth and no large chunks remain.
  4. Add the sugar: Add the sugar to the blender and blend for another 3 to 5 minutes until everything is well combined and homogeneous.
  5. Mix the dry ingredients: Transfer the blended wet mixture to a large bowl. Gradually add the all-purpose flour, mixing gently with a whisk or spatula until the batter is smooth.
  6. Add baking powder: Add the baking powder and gently fold it into the batter with a spoon or spatula (do not overmix).
  7. Bake: Pour the batter into the greased pan. Bake in the preheated oven for about 40 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let it cool slightly before adding the glaze.

For the creamy chocolate glaze

  1. Combine ingredients: In a medium saucepan, combine the sweetened condensed milk, table cream (or heavy whipping cream), chocolate powder, and butter.
  2. Cook: Place the saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir continuously with a spatula or wooden spoon to prevent it from sticking to the bottom of the pan.
  3. Find the right consistency: Keep stirring until all ingredients are well combined and the mixture thickens slightly. You want a soft, pourable consistency (it usually takes about 5 to 8 minutes).
  4. Glaze the cake: Pour the warm chocolate glaze evenly over the baked cake. Let it set slightly before serving.
